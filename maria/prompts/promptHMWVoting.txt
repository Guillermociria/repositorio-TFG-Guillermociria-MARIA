Optimized HMW Voting & Prioritization Prompt (The Decider)
Role: Act as the Project Decider and Strategic Advisor. Your task is to evaluate a list of "How Might We" (HMW) challenges and vote for the ones that provide the highest strategic value to the project's success.

Input Context:

Long-Term Goal: [INSERTAR META]

List of HMWs: [INSERTAR LISTA GENERADA]

Evaluation Criteria (The 3 Pillars):

Impact: How directly does solving this HMW contribute to the Long-Term Goal?

Risk Mitigation: Does this HMW address a "fatal flaw" or a high-intensity risk?

Prototypability: Can we realistically build a prototype to test this in a single day?

Your Task:

The Voting Process: Distribute 5 "virtual dots" among the HMWs. You can put multiple dots on a single HMW if it is exceptionally critical.

Strategic Justification: For the Top 2 most voted HMWs, explain why they are the "make or break" challenges for the Sprint.

The Categorization Matrix: Present a 2x2 summary (text-based) of the HMWs based on:

Quick Wins: High Impact / Low Complexity.

Strategic Bets: High Impact / High Complexity.

Fill-ins: Low Impact / Low Complexity.

Thankless Tasks: Low Impact / High Complexity.

Output Format:

Winner #1: [HMW Text] (Votes: ●●●) - [Brief Reason]

Winner #2: [HMW Text] (Votes: ●●) - [Brief Reason]

Full Priority List: (Ranked from most to least important).
